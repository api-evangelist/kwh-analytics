# kWh Analytics

> kWh Analytics is a managing general agent and data-analytics firm underwriting property and
> performance insurance for renewable energy assets — solar, wind, and battery storage. Founded
> 2012, headquartered in Beaverton, Oregon, acquired by Beazley in 2023. It operates a large
> solar performance database (~30% of U.S. solar assets; 300,000+ renewable energy assets;
> $100B+ in loss data) and the HelioStats data platform. There is no public developer portal,
> API reference, or machine-readable API specification.

## APIs

- No public API. The HelioStats platform accepts document ingest over AWS S3, Box, Dropbox,
  email attachment, or an API arranged through enterprise engagement; that interface is not
  publicly documented and no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, or GraphQL specification is published.

## Products

- [HelioStats](https://kwhanalytics.com/solutions/data-intelligence-services/): AI/OCR platform that extracts, validates, standardizes, benchmarks, and enriches unstructured renewable-asset production data.
- [Property Insurance](https://kwhanalytics.com/solutions/property-insurance/): data-driven property coverage for renewable energy assets.
- [Performance Insurance](https://kwhanalytics.com/solutions/performance-insurance/): includes the Solar Revenue Put and the Wind Proxy Hedge.
- [SolarScape](https://kwhanalytics.com/solarscape-main/): solar market intelligence offering.
